Frequently Asked Questions

What is your policy on outside food and drinks? +

We kindly ask that you purchase snacks and drinks from our wide selection at the MegaPlex concession stand. We offer everything from popcorn to specialty sodas to make your movie experience great!

Do you offer group rates or birthday party packages? +

Absolutely! We love hosting parties. Please visit our Special Events page for all the details on booking a great celebration with special ticket and concession deals.

How early should I arrive before my movie starts? +

We recommend arriving at least 20 minutes before the showtime listed on your ticket. This gives you plenty of time to grab your snacks, find your seat, and watch the fun trailers!

Do you have special discounts for students or seniors? +

Yes, we offer discounted tickets every weekday before 4 PM for both students and seniors. Just show your ID at the box office!
